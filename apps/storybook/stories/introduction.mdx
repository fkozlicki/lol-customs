# Derby's design system

Everything visual in Derby comes from `packages/ui` (ADR 0004), and this Storybook shows all of it and
nothing else. It has two sections:

- **Design system** — the tokens, the principles they come from, and the neutral primitives under
  `@v1/ui/*`: a button, a dialog, a table. Derby Sync uses some of these too.
- **Recipes** — Derby's own components under `@v1/ui/recipes/*`: a match card, a standings row, an
  auction lobby. Each takes plain props; the app's containers fetch and map the data.

Every component has a **Docs** page: its props, and every story with the comment that explains it.

## The toolbar

- **Theme** — dark and light. DESIGN.md judges every view in both.
- **Locale** — English and Polish; every string a recipe renders exists in both.
- **Viewport** — 390 and 1280 px, the two widths DESIGN.md judges a view at.

## Stories are tests

Every story runs as a test in CI, in each locale and theme: Vitest renders it in Chromium through this
same preview, runs its `play` function, and checks it with axe. The **Interactions** panel replays a
story's `play`, the **Accessibility** panel lists what axe finds, and the test widget at the bottom
of the sidebar runs the lot. A story fails on an accessibility violation, a `console.error`, or an
empty render. Chromatic publishes every build and diffs it visually, in both themes.

How to write one is in **Recipes › Writing recipes**; the rules behind the look, in
**Design system › Principles**.
